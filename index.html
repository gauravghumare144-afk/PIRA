ℹ️ About PRIA

PRIA stands for Principal's Responsible Intelligent Assistant.

It is a student-created web prototype designed to demonstrate how technology could organize school information in one place.

🎯 Goal

Make school information easier to organize and access.

🧠 Concept

A responsible assistant that supports—not replaces—school leadership.

🚀 Future

Authentication, real databases, role-based access and school-approved AI could be added later.


Motto: “Helping Principals Lead, Helping Students Grow.”

Prototype created as a Class 9 computer project.
